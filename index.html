Case studies

The research behind the rebuild.

Before we design anything, we audit what's actually happening — the broken fields, the manual workarounds, the tools that don't talk to each other. Here's that process, in the open.

SEE Lagos

Restaurant & lounge — Lekki Phase 1, Lagos
Live Demo +
What the research found
  • Empty fields on the live menu page — "Avg cooking time" and "Avg cost of a dish" display with no value filled in.
  • Menu prices carry odd kobo decimals (₦28,999.99, ₦26,500.01) — a sign of a bulk import or currency-conversion error, not intentional pricing.
  • Item names and category tabs get cut off mid-word on smaller screens — the template wasn't built for their actual content length.
  • Their own site lists hours as 00:00–23:59, while their Google Business Profile says Closed / Opens 8am — two sources of truth disagreeing.
  • Presence is split across Google, Instagram, a separate seelifestyle.ng domain, a third-party QR-menu tool, Glovo, and WhatsApp — none connected.
  • A manual banner asks guests to "pay ONLY into the company's bank account" — a patch for fraud risk, not a fixed payment flow.
What we built
  • One menu system with real content limits, so names and prices display fully on every screen size.
  • A single source of truth for hours and menu pricing, synced everywhere it's shown instead of edited in two places.
  • An integrated checkout (Paystack or Flutterwave) to replace manual bank transfers and the fraud-warning banner.
  • One order inbox instead of orders scattered across WhatsApp, Glovo and Instagram DMs.
  • Reviews and photos pulled live from Google instead of managed by hand.
Live demo: seelagoesdemo.netlify.app · Original site audited at app.mulle.ee/qr/seelifest, reviewed August 2026.

Maple Lagos

Restaurant, bar & wine lounge — Lagos
Live Demo +
What was wrong
  • The homepage is a black screen with just the logo — no menu, no hours, nothing else visible.
  • Everything else sits behind a hamburger icon, leading to a bare 4-link menu page, where each link opens its own long, separately-loading scroll.
  • Every price is missing three zeros — a shot of Jameson shows as ₦7.2 instead of ₦7,200. It runs through food, bar and wine, so it's a systemic export bug, not a typo.
  • Two clashing brand looks — black/gold on the homepage, cream/green script on the menu pages — read like two different businesses.
  • No visible hours, address, or way to book a table anywhere on the site.
What we built
  • One page, four tabs — Food, Bar, Wine, Dessert — with sub-filters. No hamburger, no reloading between categories.
  • Every price corrected and comma-formatted, with the pricing bug called out directly in the hero as the opening hook.
  • One unified visual identity pulling from their real brand — the black and gold, plus the forest green from their actual menu type.
  • A live before/after comparison section, so visitors can see exactly what changed and why.
  • A working reservation form.
Contact details (address, phone, booking line) don't exist anywhere on the current site — the demo shows an honest placeholder for these rather than inventing them.
What's next

The demo is Phase 1. The pitch is a second phase that turns the fix into an ongoing system.

Phase 1 — This demo
  • Single-page menu with working tabs and filters
  • Corrected, comma-formatted pricing
  • One unified brand identity
  • Reservation form
Phase 2 — Proposed
  • One dashboard to update the menu everywhere at once
  • Automatic checks that would've caught the ₦7.2 bug before it went live
  • An AI concierge chat widget for guest questions
  • WhatsApp and Instagram unified into one inbox
  • Time-of-day smart menu switching (breakfast → lunch → dinner)
  • A single view for reviews across platforms
Live demo: maplelagoesdemo.netlify.app · Original site audited at maplelagos.com, reviewed August 2026.

KOAM Star — XSTO Media

TV production & entertainment
In Production +
The role

KOAM Star's team needed someone to build and run the technical side of XSTO Media — not a one-off site, but an ongoing platform with real applicants and real data moving through it. This one is a running engagement, not a demo.

What's running
  • Audition registration, so applicants can apply directly through the platform.
  • Fan signups, building a mailing list of the show's audience.
  • Application status lookup, so applicants can check where they stand without contacting the team directly.
  • A Postgres database (Neon) holding all registration and applicant data.
  • Transactional email (Resend) handling confirmations and status updates.
  • Three serverless API routes powering registration, signups and status lookup end-to-end.
How this one's differentThe audits and demos above are the pitch. This is what happens after — Provaxis builds and maintains the platform for KOAM Star on an ongoing basis, not a single delivery.
Live platform: xstoproduction.vercel.app — in active production, August 2026.
