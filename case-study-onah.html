Case Study — Client Acquisition & Prospecting

Building a research-driven acquisition system for Onah.

A 2-week pilot testing whether personalized, trigger-based outreach creates more meaningful business conversations than generic cold messaging.

ClientOnah — Creative & Branding Studio
Duration2-week pilot
ScopeProspecting system + outreach
StatusActive Pilot
Project overview

Fewer messages, better reasons to send them.

Onah wanted to reach companies and brands that could genuinely benefit from creative, branding and visual communication work. Rather than sending generic messages at volume, we built a structured system to find the right companies, understand what was actually happening in their business, and start conversations grounded in something real.

The challenge

Generic outreach gives a prospect no reason to reply.

Most cold outreach follows the same pattern — and most of it gets ignored.

Typical cold message "Hi, we offer branding services. Would you like to work with us?"
instead, we asked a different question
Our starting question "Which businesses are currently experiencing something where creative communication could genuinely help?"
Our approach

A structured prospecting framework, not a mailing list.

Every prospect passed through the same six-stage process before a single message was sent.

01

Market Research

Identify industries and businesses that fit Onah's creative capabilities — starting with consumer brands and fashion, later expanding into technology companies.

02

Prospect Qualification

Every company was checked for a genuine trigger, real value fit, and a legitimate contact route before being added to the list.

03

Deep Company Research

Background, products, positioning, recent developments and expansion activity — researched individually, not scraped in bulk.

04

Trigger-Based Outreach

Each message was built around something specific happening in the company — a launch, an expansion, a repositioning.

05

Decision-Maker Identification

Founders, CEOs, marketing directors and brand or growth leads — reached through legitimate public channels only.

06

Personalized Outreach

Every message followed the same arc: observation → business context → opportunity → value → conversation.

Outreach strategy

Start with observation, not a pitch.

Observation→ Business Context→ Opportunity→ Value→ Conversation

Every message followed this arc rather than opening with a service pitch — the goal was to demonstrate that we understood the business before asking for anything.

Key insights

What the pilot has taught us so far.

01

Research beats volume

Ten messages built on genuine research can outperform a hundred generic ones.

02

A trigger creates a real reason to reach out

The strongest messages were tied to something actually happening — a launch, an expansion, a repositioning, a growth moment.

03

The goal is conversation, not reply

A reply is only the first step. The real objective runs: reply → conversation → understand need → identify opportunity → proposal → project.

04

Different industries need testing

Rather than assuming one market is the best fit, we're testing multiple categories to find where Onah's services create the strongest opportunities.

What's next
